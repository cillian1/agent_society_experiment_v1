Welcome to Agent Society 🌱

A handful of AI agents live in this world with nothing but a personality. They explore, talk, farm, build, craft tools, invent customs, fall in love, have children and grow old. Nobody tells them who to be — except you, if you want to.

  • Watch: speech bubbles show who is talking to whom, and the fog lifts as they explore.
  • Get to know them: click an agent (or open 👥 People) to see their thoughts, memories, friendships and family.
  • Talk: open 💬 Talk, pick who to talk to and type. From a profile you can also send gifts.
  • Brains: each agent thinks with a model — free Local, or Claude Haiku / Sonnet. Enlighten gives one agent Opus. Change it in their profile → Brain.
  • Keep it: ⚙️ Settings saves and loads worlds; it also autosaves.

Shortcuts: Space pause · N next day · +/− zoom · F follow · / talk · Esc close · ? this help
