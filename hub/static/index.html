Sol, mentor & referee

Sol reviews the society every few days (Sol decides when the next review is, and you can ask any time), judges everything agents attempt or invent, and designs blueprints for new buildings. A smarter Sol makes a richer world.

Thinking & cost

Between thoughts agents follow the steps they planned (“next”) or an obvious routine (eat, gather, tend, keep building) without a model call. Being spoken to, hunger, a baby or a failure always gets them thinking right away. At night they sleep for free; on some nights each has one dream, at a random hour, where they reflect, set ambitions, may wake up with an idea, and tidy up their memories. The rules every agent shares are cached on Claude (cached input costs a tenth).

Welcome to Agent Society 🌱

A handful of AI agents live in this world with nothing but a personality. They explore, talk, farm, build, craft tools, invent customs, fall in love, have children and grow old. Nobody tells them who to be — except you, if you want to.

  • Watch: speech bubbles show who is talking to whom, the fog lifts as they explore, and buildings rise as they work on them. One turn is one hour; they sleep at night.
  • Get to know them: click an agent (or open 👥 People) to see their thoughts, memories, friendships and family.
  • Talk: open 💬 Talk, pick who to talk to and type. From a profile you can also send gifts.
  • Families: a woman and a man in love can have a baby: 10 days of pregnancy, then a baby who needs feeding (care) for 5 days, a child for 5 more, then an adult.
  • Brains: each agent thinks with a model — free Local, or Claude Haiku / Sonnet. Enlighten gives one agent Opus. Change it in their profile → Brain.
  • Keep it: ⚙️ Settings saves and loads worlds; it also autosaves.

Shortcuts: Space pause · N next hour · +/− zoom · F follow · / talk · Esc close · ? this help
